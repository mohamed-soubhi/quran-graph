Rules used in this draft (please confirm or correct them, too):

  • QURAN: everything between ﴿ and ﴾, brackets included, with footnotes inside nested. Links: EXPLAINS = an ayah this passage explains, CITES = another ayah. Unbracketed quotes are marked only when presented as a quote (not phrases woven into prose).
  • HADITH: a narration with an isnad (حدثنا / أخبرنا / سمعت) or an explicit takhrij (رواه / أخرجه + collector), or the Prophet’s ﷺ words or acts quoted directly. Starts at the attribution cue («قال الإمام أحمد:», «وفي الحديث»), ends after the last word of the matn or the takhrij that belongs to it; closing quote marks included, the sentence-final full stop not. A takhrij sentence about another collection («وقد رواه مسلم عن … به») is its own span. A footnote after the end is not inside the hadith.
  • kind: marfu = reaches the Prophet ﷺ; athar = stops at a Companion or Successor. Both count toward the 4b targets. A dream or story report is COMMENTARY, not HADITH (decided 2026-11-03).
  • Not HADITH (left as commentary): a tafsir opinion with only a short route and no transmission verb or takhrij («قال علي بن أبي طلحة عن ابن عباس: …», «قال مجاهد: …»).
  • OTHER: poetry; one span per poem, from the first word to the last «...».
