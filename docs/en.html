Quran Tafsir Graph

A graph database that links every ayah to Ibn Kathir's tafsir, and the tafsir to its evidence (other ayat and hadith), so every link can be traced back to its source.

Ayah→ Tafsir passage Ibn Kathir→ Evidence a cited ayahor Hadith with its grade from the source

The idea

Ibn Kathir's tafsir is a long text (13.4 million characters) that mixes explanation with other ayat, hadith, reports of the Companions and editor footnotes. We never change a character of it. Instead we add a layer of structure on top: every part of the text is marked by its exact character offsets, then linked to the ayah or hadith it refers to.

The graph can then answer questions such as: which hadith did Ibn Kathir cite for this ayah? Where does a given hadith appear across the whole tafsir? Which ayat did he use to explain the Quran by the Quran? Every answer comes with its path to the source.

  • 1Language models only extract and link; they never write tafsir and never grade hadith.
  • 2Hadith grades come from the source, unchanged, with the grader's name.
  • 3The original tafsir text is never cut or edited; structure is added as character offsets.
  • 4Every link carries its source, method and confidence.
  • 5Raw source files are read-only, each with a SHA-256 checksum.
  • 6A phase starts only after the previous one passes a measured gate signed off by Aya & Mohamed.
Why a graph? Here the links are the information: one ayah explained by another, one hadith cited in dozens of places. Neo4j makes those paths directly queryable.

Quran text from Tanzil (Uthmani and simple spelling); words, roots and lemmas from the Quranic Arabic Corpus.

L1Tafsir
MufassirTafsirBookTafsirSegment 1,911COVERS 6,236

Ibn Kathir in 1,911 passages as in the source; each passage is stored once and linked to every ayah it covers.

L2Evidence
SpanHadith 36,512HadithBook 10Grader 9Narrator

Marked parts of the tafsir (Quran, hadith, footnote, commentary), linked to ayat and hadith, and later to narrator chains.

L3Semantics
TopicConceptABOUT

Topics and concepts (people, places, rulings) with embeddings for search by meaning.

Real counts Every number is measured from the data loaded in the graph, and checked by tests after each load.
