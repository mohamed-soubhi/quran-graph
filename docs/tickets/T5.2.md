# T5.2 — Explicit hadith links from the editor's footnote numbers (agy)

**Goal:** link HADITH spans to `Hadith` nodes when the editor's footnote gives a collection and a number, such as «صحيح البخاري برقم (٤٧٧٧)».
These links are the strongest evidence we have. The step is deterministic and uses no LLM.
agy builds the JSONL files, a loader and tests, then **stops**. **Claude writes `CITES` to the real Neo4j** after Aya & Mohamed approve.

## Decision (Aya & Mohamed, 2026-11-17)
- **D1. Muslim: link all narrations of the cited number.** «صحيح مسلم برقم (٢٨٦٥)» cites Abd al-Baqi number 2865. In our source that number can have several entries (`arabic_number` `"2865.01"`, `"2865.02"`, …), which are the same hadith through different chains. Link the span to **every** entry whose `arabic_number` is `2865` or starts with `2865.`. Nothing is guessed and nothing goes to review.

## Steps
1. **Footnote references** (new `src/extract/hadith_refs.py`, pure functions).
   a. `parse_refs(footnote_text) -> list[Ref]`. A Ref has `collection`, `number` (ASCII digits) and `ref_text` (the matched text). Convert Arabic-Indic digits.
      Collection patterns, tried in this order. A footnote position already matched is not matched again.
      - `nasai_kubra`: «سنن النسائي الكبرى/الكبري» | «النسائي في (السنن)? الكبرى/الكبري» | «السنن الكبرى للنسائي»
      - `bukhari`: «صحيح البخاري» | «البخاري في صحيحه» | «البخاري»
      - `muslim`: «صحيح مسلم» | «مسلم في صحيحه» | «مسلم»
      - `abudawud`: «سنن أبي داود» | «أبو داود في (السنن|سننه)» | «أبو داود»
      - `tirmidhi`: «سنن الترمذي» | «الترمذي في (السنن|سننه|جامعه)» | «الترمذي»
      - `ibnmajah`: «سنن ابن ماجه/ماجة» | «ابن ماجه/ماجة في (السنن|سننه)» | «ابن ماجه/ماجة»
      - `nasai`: «سنن النسائي» | «النسائي في (السنن|سننه)» | «النسائي»

      Each pattern is followed by `\s+برقم\s*\(` and a number list `N(، N)*` and `)`, then optionally more `وبرقم (…)` groups.
      Do not match a name that is part of a longer word: «للبخاري» is not «البخاري».
   b. `owner(footnote)`: the HADITH span id, by the inside / after rule in Facts, or `None`. Inside wins.
   c. `resolve(ref)`:
      - Muslim: every hadith whose `arabic_number` is `N` or starts with `N.` (D1).
      - nasai_kubra: nothing, with reason `not_in_sources`.
      - Others: `<collection>:<N>` if it exists.
      - No hit gives reason `not_found`.
2. **Build** `data/interim/links/footnote_links.jsonl`. Write one row per distinct (span, hadith) pair, sorted:
   `{"span_id", "hadith_id", "evidence": [footnote span_ids], "ref_text": [..], "number_kind": "hadithnumber"|"arabicnumber", "outcome": "auto", "method": "regex", "confidence": 0.95, "source": "run:<yyyymmdd-HHMM>-footnote-links", "text_sim": <float>}`.
   - `text_sim` is information only. Use 4-gram containment on `text_norm` with spaces removed and footnotes taken out of the span text: |A∩B| / min(|A|,|B|). It never decides anything in T5.2. T5.3/G5 use it to study errors.
   - Write every reference that gives no link to `data/interim/links/footnote_refs_unlinked.jsonl`: `{"footnote_id", "span_id" (or null), "collection", "number", "ref_text", "reason": "not_in_sources"|"not_found"|"unattached"}`.
     `unattached` means the footnote has no HADITH owner. Those 881+34 references are inputs for T5.4/T5.5, which may find missed hadith spans.
   - Print the counts in the shape of the Facts table, plus the totals. **Explain any difference from Claude's reference numbers over 2 %.**
3. **Loader** `write_cites(driver, path)` in `src/graph/hadith_links.py`:
   `MERGE (s:Span {span_id})-[r:CITES]->(h:Hadith {hadith_id})` and set `evidence`, `ref_text`, `number_kind`, `outcome`, `method`, `source`, `confidence` and `text_sim`.
   - It must refuse a missing or empty file, and refuse rows whose span or hadith does not exist (count them; Claude expects 0).
   - Before writing, delete existing `CITES` edges from HADITH spans whose `method` is `regex` and whose `source` starts with `run:` and contains `footnote-links`. This keeps the write idempotent.
   - Do **not** touch `CITES` from QURAN spans.
3b. **Task:** in `scripts/tasks.py`, add `links`. It builds and prints the counts; `links --write` also writes to Neo4j.
4. **Tests** (`tests/test_hadith_links.py`):
   - Parse every form in the Facts table, including the two multi-number forms and `«ماجة»/«ماجه»`. Kubra is never `nasai`. «للبخاري برقم (١)» gives no ref.
   - The owner rule works for inside, for inside through a QURAN parent, for after-with-only-punctuation, and gives `None` when a word lies between.
   - The Muslim footnote `2865` resolves to all its `arabic_number` 2865.* entries, and the set includes `muslim:7207`.
   - The build totals equal the numbers printed by step 2. Pin them as exact numbers in the test.
   - Writer test on a fixture only: span ids starting with `test:` and hadith ids `test-coll:*`. Write twice and check the count does not change; then delete only the fixture. Skip if Neo4j is not reachable.
5. Run `python scripts/tasks.py test`; every test must pass. Update `docs/schema.md`: `CITES` (Span→Hadith) `evidence` is a **list** of footnote span ids, and add `ref_text`, `number_kind`, `outcome`, `text_sim`.
   Add a dated entry to `docs/decisions.md` (D1 and the counts). Write the report `docs/tickets/reports/T5.2.md` (Deviations first). Set the TRACKER row to `review` and commit with a message starting `T5.2:`.
   **Do not push and do not run `links --write`.**
