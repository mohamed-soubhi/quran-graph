# T5.1 Report — Load hadith collections (L2) into Neo4j

## Deviations
- **Float hadithnumber counts across collections:**
  - *Ticket statement:* "hadithnumber is an int, except 26 Bukhari values that are floats (e.g. 402.2)" and "26 float numbers" in acceptance test criteria.
  - *Observed in raw data:* Bukhari indeed contains exactly 26 float values (e.g. `402.2`), but float `hadithnumber` values also exist in three other collections:
    - `bukhari`: 26 floats
    - `ibnmajah`: 2 floats (`3930.2`, `4089.2`)
    - `nasai`: 7 floats (`384.2`, `434.2`, `2821.2`, `3533.2`, `3533.3`)
    - `tirmidhi`: 35 floats (`815.2`, `1114.2`, `1185.2`, `1424.2`, `1425.2`, etc.)
    Total loaded float numbers: **70** across all 10 collections (plus 7 float numbers among the 74 empty-text Tirmidhi entries).
  - *Action taken:* Rather than coercing floats in other collections to integers (which would corrupt identifiers or cause collisions), `format_hadith_number` preserves string representations for all floats (`str(float)`). In `tests/test_hadith_load.py`, we assert both: exactly 26 floats in Bukhari, and exactly 70 floats in total across all loaded hadith.

1. **Parser (`src/ingest/parse_hadith.py`):**
   - Parses the 10 pinned fawazahmed0 Arabic editions in `data/raw/hadith/fawazahmed0/` (`ara-<collection>.json`, commit `df57907`).

3. **Schema & Indexes (`src/graph/schema.cypher`):**

5. **Documentation:**
   - `docs/schema.md`: Documented `HadithBook.name_en`, `Hadith.text_norm`, and `hadith_text_norm_ft`.
   - `docs/decisions.md`: Added dated decision recording all measured counts.
