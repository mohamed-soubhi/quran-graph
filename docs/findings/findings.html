A. Tafsir Ibn Kathir — the edition

A1. Written Quran references that point to the wrong ayah edition

The editors print a reference after many Quran quotes, e.g. ﴿…﴾ «[الْأَنْبِيَاءِ: ٣٥]». We checked all 5,164 printed references against the quoted words. In 40 the words were not found in the referenced ayah. One of them was our own error, since fixed (E1); the other 39, after reading each one:

  • 34 printing errors: one wrong digit («[الْأَنْبِيَاءِ: ٢٥]» for 21:35), a missing digit («[النِّسَاءِ: ١١]» for 4:110), or a wrong surah name («[الزُّمَرِ: ١٨]» for المزمل 73:18, «[النَّمْلِ: ١٠]» for النحل 16:10);
  • 3 near-identical ayat where the reference may be what Ibn Kathir meant (2:126 بلدا / 14:35 البلد);
  • 2 references to the start of a passage rather than the exact ayah.

→ Full list of the 39 cases with texts and suggested corrections (also reference-typos.csv).

Second source check (2026-11-03, sample of 3 of the 39): the Arabic Ibn Kathir on quran.com (tafsir 14, read through quran-mcp) prints the same wrong reference in all 3: 10:87 (words of 2:153, printed [البقرة : 156]), 11:94 (words of 26:187, printed [الشعراء : 189]) and 7:123 (words of 20:71, printed [طه : 70]). These 3 are therefore not copying errors made by our download (spa5k): the two digital texts share them, although they differ in format (quran.com has no diacritics, no [[…]] notes, and uses ( ) around quotes). We have not compared a printed volume, so the origin of the errors is still unconfirmed, and the other 36 cases are unchecked.

Action: the graph links the quote to the ayah that holds the words, keeps the printed reference on the link (ref_text) and lowers confidence to 0.9. The tafsir text is never changed.

A2. Quotes follow the "simple" spelling, not the mushaf spelling edition

Ibn Kathir quotes «الْعَالَمِينَ», not the Uthmani «ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ». Even without diacritics, Tanzil's Uthmani and Simple texts differ in 3,777 ayat, so quotes are matched against the Simple text. Beyond that, 122 quotes differ from Tanzil Simple in the rasm of a word:

Action: a spelling-tolerant key for quotes of 3+ words, confidence: 0.8.

A3. Hamza seats differ between the tafsir and Tanzil edition

Ibn Kathir «أَئِنَّا» vs Tanzil Simple «أإنا» (56:47). After Normalizer v2, about 200 links still needed a key that ignores ا ئ ؤ.

Action: loose key, confidence: 0.9.

A4. Edition quirks inside the quote brackets edition

126 of 26,686 quotes (0.5 %) stay unlinked. Besides one-word glosses of ayat the passage does not cover (left unlinked on purpose — one word is too ambiguous), the edition has:

  • ayah numbers inside the brackets: «…يُنفِقُونَ ٣﴾», «فَيُوَفِّيهِمْ (١) أُجُورَهُمْ»;
  • a written reference inside the closing bracket: «…[الْوَاقِعَةِ: ٣٨، ٣٩] ﴾»;
  • two words joined: «فَأَمَّاالَّذِينَ» (3:7); a doubled letter: «مممن» (2:282);
  • a stray digit inside a surah name: «[٠الْفُرْقَانِ: ٧٢]».

A5. Editor footnotes [[…]] edition

31,203 footnotes in 1,857 of the 1,911 passages; 110 run over several lines; the longest has 7,075 characters (ibn-kathir:33:41-44:1). 635 footnotes sit inside a Quran quote, and quotes sit inside hadith — up to three levels (2:255: a footnote inside a quote inside Anas's hadith).

Action: schema v1.1 C1 — nested spans with INSIDE.

A6. The raw files repeat each passage once per ayah edition

One file per ayah (6,236), but a passage covering several ayat is copied into each: 48.2 M characters in total, 13.4 M distinct, in 1,911 passages. The largest covers 74:11–30 (20 ayat).

Action: one TafsirSegment per passage, linked to each ayah with COVERS.

B. Quran text and morphology

B1. Tanzil puts the basmala at the start of ayah 1 in 112 surahs

In both Uthmani and Simple-Clean, except surahs 1 and 9. In the Hafs count the basmala is an ayah only in 1:1.

Action: the loader removes it and sets Surah.has_basmala (schema Q1).

B2. The Quranic Corpus joins two words in 4 ayat

«بَعْدَ مَا» in 2:181, 8:6, 13:37 and «إِلْ يَاسِينَ» in 37:130 are one word in the corpus, two in Tanzil.

B3. Hamza / tatweel spelling between the corpus and Tanzil

319 of 77,383 aligned words differed only in hamza seat or tatweel (corpus «ٱلْءَاخِرَةِ» vs Tanzil «ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ», 2:4). After Normalizer v2: 2 (12:39 and 12:41, «يَٰصَىٰحِبَىِ» vs «يَٰصَٰحِبَىِ», a genuine variant).

B4. Words with two stems or two roots

486 corpus words have two stems (e.g. «مِمَّا» = مِن + ما) and so two lemmas; one word has two roots (20:94, «يَبْنَؤُمَّ»); 175 lemmas have no root.

C. Hadith collections (fawazahmed0)

C1. Ibn Kathir's Muslim numbers are the source's arabicnumber, not hadithnumber

Footnote «صحيح مسلم برقم (٢٨٦٥)» = the entry with arabicnumber 2865.01 (hadithnumber 7207). hadithnumber 2865 is a different hadith.

Action: Phase 5 links footnote numbers through arabic_number.

C2. No grades for Bukhari and Muslim in this source

21,185 of 36,512 entries carry a grade from 9 named graders; Bukhari, Muslim and the 40-hadith books have none.

Action: the graph stores "no grade in source"; it never infers a grade from the collection name (hard rule 2).

C3. 408 entries with empty text

Muslim 203, Nasai 86, Tirmidhi 74, Malik 29, Bukhari 9, Ibn Majah 5, Abu Dawud 2 — mostly introduction placeholders. Kept as they are, never filled.

C4. Musnad Ahmad is missing

Ibn Kathir cites «قال الإمام أحمد» constantly (3 of the 24 gold hadith spans). Its isnads are still kept (schema C2: narrators attach to the HADITH span) but there is no Hadith node to link to.

D. Annotation (gold set, Phase 4)

D1. What counts as a hadith span decided by Aya & Mohamed

  • Athar (stopping at a Companion or Successor) are HADITH spans (kind: athar) and count toward the 4b targets.
  • A dream or story report with an isnad is commentary, not hadith (gold W44, Ibn Asakir).
  • A takhrij line without its own chain («وكذلك رواه ابن جرير من حديث حمزة الزيات») belongs to the hadith before it; one with its own chain («وقد رواه مسلم عن أبي بكر … عن الجريري به») is a separate span.
  • kind follows what the quoted route says («عن علي مرفوعا» → marfu), even when Ibn Kathir then prefers the mawquf version («وهو أشبه») — weighing versions is grading.

D2. Human review of the draft

50 windows, 177 draft spans: Mohamed marked 172 correct and 5 wrong (all resolved); one window had to be widened because it started in the middle of a hadith.

D3. Language models and character offsets

GLM returns valid structured output (5/5 runs) and finds both hadith in 2:255, but its character offsets move by a few characters between runs, and it spends ~67 s per paragraph counting characters.

Action: Phase 4b asks the model for short text anchors and locates them in code.

E. Our own errors found by checking

E1. The reference check misses a dropped «ف» fixed in T4.4b

﴿وَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِلْمُكَذِّبِينَ﴾ [الطُّورِ: ١١]

In the tafsir of 14:47–48 the reference is right (52:11 «فَوَيْلٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ لِلْمُكَذِّبِينَ»), but the check did not allow the missing «ف» and moved the link to 77:15.

Fix: the reference check must accept a dropped or added و ف ب ل at the start of the quote, as the matcher already does elsewhere. Done in T4.4b: the quote links to 52:11 again (regex, 1.0).

E2. The gold set alone could not prove the Quran matcher changed how we check

The gold Quran links were drafted by a matcher similar to the one being tested, so 79/79 on the gold set proved less than it seemed. A whole-book check found A1–A4.

Action: every extractor is also checked on the whole book, not only on the gold set.
